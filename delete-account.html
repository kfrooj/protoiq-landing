Delete your ProtoIQ account

ProtoIQ users can delete their account and app data from inside the app.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open ProtoIQ.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

What data is deleted?

Deleting your account removes your ProtoIQ app data, including your profile, protocols, injection history, skipped records, reminders, adherence data, and inventory tracking data.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app and need help deleting your account, email: support@protoiq.uk

Data retention

Once deletion is confirmed, ProtoIQ removes your app data from our active database. Some limited records may remain temporarily in backups or logs where required for security, troubleshooting, or legal compliance.
