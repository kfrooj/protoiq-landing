ProtoIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

ProtoIQ is a protocol tracking application designed to help users manage schedules, reminders, inventory, injection history, adherence tracking, and related protocol data.

Information we collect

ProtoIQ may collect and store:

  • Email address used for authentication
  • Protocol and schedule information
  • Injection history and skipped records
  • Inventory and adherence tracking data
  • Notification preferences
  • Basic device and diagnostic information

How we use your information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide app functionality
  • Send reminder and account emails
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Support account recovery and troubleshooting
  • Maintain app security and prevent abuse

Notifications and emails

ProtoIQ may send protocol reminders, account notifications, password reset emails, and service-related communications.

Data storage

User data is securely stored using third-party infrastructure providers, including authentication, database, hosting, and email service providers.

Account deletion

Users can delete their account and associated app data directly within the app through the Settings screen.

For more information, visit the Delete Account page.

Third-party services

ProtoIQ may use trusted third-party providers for infrastructure and app operations, including hosting, authentication, analytics, notifications, and email delivery.

Medical disclaimer

ProtoIQ is intended for informational and tracking purposes only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact: support@protoiq.uk
